# Project Overview

## Project Title
Customer Churn Analytics & Prediction Dashboard

## Business Problem
Banks often lose customers due to various factors such as low engagement, product mismatch, or customer dissatisfaction. Customer churn can reduce profitability and increase the cost of acquiring new customers.

## Project Goal
The goal of this project is to analyze customer churn behavior, identify the main drivers behind churn, and build a simple predictive model to estimate churn risk. The final results will also be presented in an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## Target Variable
The target variable is `Exited`, which indicates whether the customer left the bank or stayed.

## Key Objectives
- Understand the customer dataset
- Clean and prepare the data
- Explore churn patterns and drivers
- Build a simple churn prediction model
- Design a dashboard for business stakeholders

## Business Questions
- What percentage of customers have churned?
- Which customer groups are more likely to leave?
- Does geography affect churn?
- Does the number of products affect churn?
- Are inactive customers more likely to churn?
- Can we predict churn using customer attributes?

## Stakeholders
- Bank management
- Customer retention team
- Business analysts
- Decision-makers
